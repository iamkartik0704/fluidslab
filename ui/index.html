METHOD · LIMITATIONS · FRONT

ABOUT

Method. Volume-of-fluid two-phase incompressible flow on a MAC grid: donor–acceptor VOF advection (Hirt & Nichols 1981), first-order upwind momentum advection, projection method (Chorin), IC(0)-preconditioned conjugate gradient for the pressure Poisson equation, explicit Euler time integration.

Known limitations. Two-dimensional only. First-order interface and momentum advection: strongly diffusive. No surface tension (CSF coefficient reserved but disabled). Open top boundary. Runs auto-pause at t* = 4.0 (validated window).

Front position. The marker shows the solver's reported front: sub-cell interpolation of the α = 0.5 crossing in the bottom cell row. The alternative "99% cumulative" definition reports the x where cumulative fluid area to the left reaches 99% of total fluid area. Both curves are drawn; the plot states which is selected.
